Releases

Why breaks

Long stretches at a desk load the same places over and over — neck, shoulders, hands, back. Short movement breaks interrupt that load before it piles up into the day.

Deskercise’s exercises are shortened adaptations of workplace physiotherapy materials. The sixty seconds are a break window that includes rest — not a stretch held for the whole timer.

  • Newcastle Hospitals NHS — 1 minute body check Quick seated checks for desk workers.
  • CCOHS — Stretching at the workstation Workstation stretches while you stay at your seat.
  • Mersey Care NHS — Desk-based jobs Movement advice for people in desk-based roles.

The 45-minute interval and 60-second window are product choices, not recommendations from these organizations.

Commands

Two things you run after install.

deskercise start

Keep the terminal open. Breaks arrive on your interval; confirm when the minute ends.

deskercise report

Opens this week’s offline HTML from ~/.deskercise.

Sample report

On your machine

Local history and the HTML report stay under ~/.deskercise. No accounts, no telemetry, no data shared.

No network requests while you run breaks or open the report — install only talks to npm.

Deskercise is a habit log, not medical advice. Stop any movement that causes pain.
